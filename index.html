Modern, Gothic romantic fantasy, woven with Northern mythology, complex female characters, and the question of how we learn to live with the ghosts of our past.

Book one

A Blood Red Heart

For some women, the greatest act of resistance is survival.

Legend tells that every five hundred years, west of the Pennine Mountains, the Beloved is born: a woman chosen to bring balance to the secret world that exists alongside our own. The Beloved has now come again, but this time she has been born on the wrong side of the mountains.

Lonely, exhausted and carrying a dark secret, Dr Charlie Hart can’t remember her past, but it refuses to forget her. Chance brings Charlie to Rowans Holt, an ancient house owned by brilliant recluse Sally Marshall, where she finds unexpected friendship and sanctuary. But when the manipulative Marcus Rosendale threatens to destroy everything, Charlie’s secret awakens.

Book two

A Blood Red Mind

Two families, one feud: a thousand years of blood.

Beautiful but isolated Laura Beaulouf is drawn tighter into the web woven by the cunning Marcus Rosendale. Does Marcus seek redemption or revenge? Meanwhile, Mac has a plan to save the Beaulouf fortunes, even if this involves breaking the heart of the woman he truly loves.

Charlie Hart knows that something was placed inside her head when she was eleven years old, but she will die if she tries to discover what it is.

Book three

A Blood Red Soul

Sometimes it is not the nature of the sacrifice that matters, but the purity of the heart that makes it.

Charlie Hart comes into her birthright of blood, and the long-predicted storm begins. Can Mac break the curse of a thousand years by completing the Beaulouf penance? And will anyone stop Marcus Rosendale in his relentless pursuit of money, power and death?

For some women, the greatest act of resistance is survival… for others it is all-out war.

About the fair

Jane Croft, writing as J. C. O’Neill

I live, work and write in Sheffield, but I was born in Cumbria, so I’m a true Northerner. I have degrees in law and medicine, and worked in the National Health Service for thirty-two years.

I began writing to amuse a much-loved relative after their cancer diagnosis. A few chapters turned into two series of books. I didn’t mean to write a modern Gothic fantasy trilogy; it wrote me.

Three kids, one dog, a Mr O’Neill, and a fondness for McVitie’s Chocolate Digestives.

I also write the Lady Emmeline Regency adventures as J. C. Richmond, and the songs for The Junos, a country musical in development in Sheffield.

Northern Gothic

A letter every month or so: a lighthearted look at a Northern Gothic building, a review of a tea shop or hostelry, and a catch-up on what I’m writing.
